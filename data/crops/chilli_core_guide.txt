=== CHILLI CORE GUIDE ===
Purpose: AI Krushi Mitra Knowledge Base

1. Crop overview
Chilli is an important spice crop grown for fresh and dry use.

2. Lifecycle stages
Seedling → Vegetative → Flowering → Fruit development → Maturity

3. Sowing time
- Nursery sowing: June
- Transplanting: July

4. Fertilizer basics
- Nitrogen for leaf growth
- Potassium for fruit quality

5. Water needs
- Light but frequent irrigation
- Avoid water stagnation

6. Common pests
- Thrips
- Mites
- Aphids

7. Common diseases
- Leaf curl virus
- Anthracnose
- Powdery mildew

8. Harvesting
- Green chilli: 60–70 days
- Dry chilli: 90–120 days

9. Storage
- Dry chilli to low moisture before storage

10. Best practices
- Use disease-free seedlings
- Mulching reduces weed growth
