              === MAIZE CORE GUIDE ===
Purpose: AI Krushi Mitra Knowledge Base

1. Crop overview
Maize is a fast-growing cereal crop used for food, fodder, and industry.

2. Lifecycle stages
Germination → Vegetative → Tasseling → Silking → Grain filling → Maturity

3. Sowing time
- Kharif: June to July
- Rabi: October to November

4. Fertilizer basics
- Nitrogen is most important
- Apply phosphorus at sowing

5. Water needs
- Regular irrigation during flowering
- Avoid drought stress

6. Common pests
- Stem borer
- Fall armyworm

7. Common diseases
- Leaf blight
- Downy mildew

8. Harvesting
- Harvest when cobs turn yellow and dry

9. Storage
- Dry grains to safe moisture before storage

10. Best practices
- Use quality hybrid seeds
- Timely weed control
