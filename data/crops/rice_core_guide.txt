                      === RICE CORE GUIDE ===
1. Crop Overview

Rice is a major food crop grown in irrigated and rainfed conditions. Proper planning, seed quality, and water management are key to high yield.

2. Crop Lifecycle

Stages
Land preparation
Seed selection
Sowing / Transplanting
Vegetative growth
Flowering
Grain filling
Maturity & harvesting
Storage

3. Seed & Variety Selection

Good seed should be
Clean (no stones, weed seeds)
Pure (single variety)
Healthy and disease free
Variety selection depends on
Water availability
Soil type
Duration of crop
Short duration: <120 days
Medium duration: 120–140 days
Long duration: >160 days

4. Land Preparation

Level the field properly
Repair bunds and remove weeds
Puddle soil if possible
Prepare seedbed for good root growth

5. Sowing

Methods
Direct seeding
Transplanting from nursery
Best practices
Plant on time
Maintain proper spacing
Avoid overcrowding

6. Fertilizer Management

Basic nutrients
Nitrogen (N)
Phosphorus (P)
Potassium (K)
Sulphur (S)
Zinc (Zn)
Key rules
Apply basal dose before sowing
Do not overuse nitrogen
Foliar spray of DAP / Urea during flowering if needed

7. Water Management

Critical stages
Seedling stage
Flowering stage
Grain filling stage
Good practices
Avoid water stagnation
Maintain good-quality water
Rice is sensitive to salinity

8. Weed Management

Methods
Hand weeding at 15–30 days
Pre-emergence herbicide if needed
Importance
Weeds compete for nutrients
Early control improves yield

9. Pest Management

Common pests
Stem borer
Leaf folder
Aphids
Brown planthopper
Integrated Pest Management (IPM)
Use resistant varieties
Encourage natural enemies
Avoid unnecessary pesticide use
Use traps when possible

10. Disease Management

Common diseases
Blast
Sheath blight
Bacterial leaf blight
Viral mosaic
Control methods
Use clean seed
Rogue infected plants
Seed treatment before sowing
Spray only when needed

11. Harvesting

Right time to harvest
80–85% grains turn straw color
Moisture content ~21–22%
Harvesting tips
Do not delay harvest
Avoid grain shattering

12. Storage

Storage rules
Dry grains to 10% moisture
Store in clean bags
Protect from insects and rats
Label bags with date & variety

13. Government & Best Practices

General recommendations
Use certified seed
Follow crop calendar
Apply fertilizers based on soil test
Use modern advisory apps
Follow government crop insurance & subsidy schemes
