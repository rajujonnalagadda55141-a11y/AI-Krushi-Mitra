                     === COTTON CORE GUIDE ===
Purpose: AI Krushi Mitra Knowledge Base

1. Crop overview
Cotton is a major fiber crop grown in warm climates with moderate rainfall.

2. Lifecycle stages
Seed germination → Vegetative growth → Flowering → Boll formation → Maturity

3. Sowing time
- Kharif season: June to July

4. Fertilizer basics
- Nitrogen promotes growth
- Phosphorus supports roots
- Potassium improves boll quality

5. Water needs
- Moderate irrigation
- Avoid waterlogging

6. Common pests
- Bollworm
- Aphids
- Whitefly

7. Common diseases
- Wilt
- Leaf curl virus
- Root rot

8. Harvesting
- Pick when bolls open fully
- Avoid picking in wet weather

9. Storage
- Store cotton in dry ventilated place

10. Best practices
- Use pest-resistant varieties
- Practice crop rotation
