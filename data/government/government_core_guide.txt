================================================
2. MAJOR AGRICULTURE SCHEMES IN INDIA
================================================

A. PMFBY – Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana  
Purpose: Crop insurance  
Benefit: Compensation for crop loss due to drought, flood, pests, disease.

B. PM-KISAN  
Purpose: Income support  
Benefit: ₹6000 per year directly to farmer bank account.

C. Soil Health Card Scheme  
Purpose: Soil testing and nutrient advice  
Benefit: Fertilizer use based on soil report.

D. Kisan Credit Card (KCC)  
Purpose: Easy farm loans  
Benefit: Low-interest credit for seeds, fertilizers, equipment.

E. PMKSY – Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchai Yojana  
Purpose: Irrigation support  
Benefit: Drip and sprinkler subsidy.

F. e-NAM – National Agriculture Market  
Purpose: Online crop selling platform  
Benefit: Better price discovery.

4. GOVERNMENT BEST PRACTICES FOR FARMERS
================================================

Seed  
- Use certified seeds only  
- Do not reuse diseased seed  

Soil  
- Test soil every 2–3 years  
- Add organic matter  

Water  
- Use drip or sprinkler irrigation  
- Avoid water wastage  

Fertilizers  
- Follow Soil Health Card  
- Avoid excess urea  

Pest & Disease  
- Follow Integrated Pest Management (IPM)  
- Use chemicals only when needed
