=== WEATHER & CLIMATE ADVISORY CORE GUIDE ===
Purpose: AI Krushi Mitra Knowledge Base
Scope: All crops and regions

================================================
1. WHY WEATHER MATTERS IN FARMING
================================================
Weather directly affects:
- Sowing time
- Crop growth
- Pest and disease spread
- Irrigation needs
- Harvesting and storage

Correct weather-based decisions increase yield and reduce losses.

================================================
2. IMPORTANT WEATHER FACTORS
================================================

Rainfall  
- Determines sowing time  
- Affects irrigation planning  

Temperature  
- Controls crop growth speed  
- High temperature increases water need  

Humidity  
- High humidity increases disease risk  

Wind  
- Helps or harms pollination  
- Can spread pests and diseases  

Sunlight  
- Essential for photosynthesis  

================================================
3. SEASONS IN INDIAN AGRICULTURE
================================================

Kharif season  
- June to October  
- Depends on monsoon rainfall  

Rabi season  
- October to March  
- Grown with residual soil moisture  

Zaid season  
- March to June  
- Short duration crops  

================================================
4. WEATHER-BASED FARM OPERATIONS
================================================

Sowing  
- Sow after first good rain  
- Avoid sowing before heavy rain forecast  

Irrigation  
- Skip irrigation if rain expected  
- Increase irrigation during heat waves  

Fertilizer application  
- Do not apply before rain  
- Apply in cool hours  

Pesticide spraying  
- Do not spray on windy or rainy days  
- Spray early morning or evening  

Harvesting  
- Avoid harvest during rain  
- Dry produce in sunlight  

================================================
5. CLIMATE RISKS TO CROPS
================================================

Drought  
- Reduces yield  
- Causes crop stress  

Flood  
- Causes root rot  
- Spreads diseases  

Heat wave  
- Reduces flowering  
- Causes fruit drop  

Cold wave  
- Damages seedlings  
- Slows growth  

Cyclone  
- Lodging of crops  
- Crop destruction  

================================================
6. CLIMATE-SMART FARMING PRACTICES
================================================

- Use drought-tolerant varieties  
- Use short-duration crops  
- Practice mulching  
- Improve soil organic matter  
- Harvest rainwater  
- Use micro-irrigation  
- Diversify crops  

================================================
7. WEATHER-BASED CROP ADVISORY
================================================

During heavy rain  
- Improve drainage  
- Delay fertilizer application  
- Watch for fungal diseases  

During drought  
- Use mulching  
- Reduce plant population  
- Provide life-saving irrigation  

During heat wave  
- Increase irrigation frequency  
- Use shade nets for vegetables  

During cold wave  
- Use light irrigation at night  
- Cover nursery beds  

================================================
8. WEATHER & DISEASE CONNECTION
================================================
High-risk conditions:
- High humidity + warm temperature → fungal diseases  
- Continuous rain → bacterial diseases  
- Dry + warm → insect outbreaks  

Farmers should increase field monitoring during such conditions.

================================================

================================================
10. FARMER WEATHER SAFETY
================================================

During lightning  
- Do not stand under trees  
- Avoid open fields  

During heat  
- Drink water frequently  
- Avoid working at noon  

During cyclone  
- Secure farm tools  
- Move animals to safe place  

================================================
11. POST-HARVEST WEATHER CARE
================================================
- Dry grains properly  
- Protect stored produce from moisture  
- Use moisture-proof storage  
- Check stores after rain
