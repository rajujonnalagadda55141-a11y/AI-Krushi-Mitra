=== DISEASES CORE GUIDE ===
Purpose: AI Krushi Mitra Knowledge Base
Scope: All major crops

================================================
1. WHAT ARE PLANT DISEASES
================================================
Plant diseases are caused by fungi, bacteria, viruses, and nematodes.  
They reduce crop yield, quality, and farmer income.

================================================
2. TYPES OF PLANT DISEASES
================================================

A. Fungal diseases  
- Blast  
- Rust  
- Powdery mildew  
- Leaf spot  
- Wilt  

B. Bacterial diseases  
- Bacterial leaf blight  
- Soft rot  
- Canker  

C. Viral diseases  
- Leaf curl  
- Mosaic  
- Yellow vein disease  

D. Nematode diseases  
- Root knot  
- Cyst nematode  

================================================
3. COMMON SYMPTOMS
================================================

Leaf symptoms  
- Yellowing  
- Spots  
- Curling  
- Drying  

Stem symptoms  
- Cracks  
- Rotting  
- Weak stems  

Root symptoms  
- Swelling  
- Rot  
- Poor root growth  

Fruit symptoms  
- Black spots  
- Rot  
- Deformation

================================================
6. DISEASE PREVENTION (MOST IMPORTANT)
================================================

1. Use certified disease-free seed  
2. Follow crop rotation  
3. Maintain field hygiene  
4. Remove infected plants  
5. Avoid overcrowding  
6. Proper drainage  
7. Balanced fertilizer use  

================================================
7. INTEGRATED DISEASE MANAGEMENT (IDM)
================================================

IDM means using all methods together:

A. Cultural control  
- Crop rotation  
- Proper spacing  
- Timely sowing  

B. Mechanical control  
- Removing infected parts  
- Destroying diseased plants  

C. Biological control  
- Using beneficial microbes  
- Trichoderma, Pseudomonas  

D. Chemical control  
- Use fungicides only when needed  
- Follow label instructions  
- Do not overuse  

================================================
8. COMMON DISEASES & SIMPLE CONTROL METHODS
================================================

Blast (Rice)  
- Avoid excess nitrogen  
- Use resistant varieties  
- Keep field clean  

Wilt (Cotton)  
- Rotate crops  
- Improve soil drainage  
- Use healthy seedlings  

Leaf blight (Maize)  
- Avoid water stagnation  
- Remove infected leaves  

Leaf curl (Chilli)  
- Control whiteflies  
- Use virus-free seedlings  
- Remove infected plants early  

================================================
9. SEED TREATMENT
================================================
Seed treatment helps prevent early diseases.

Methods  
- Hot water treatment  
- Bio-agent coating  
- Chemical seed dressing  

Benefits  
- Protects seedlings  
- Improves germination  
- Reduces disease risk  

================================================
10. WEATHER & DISEASE LINK
================================================
High risk conditions:
- High humidity  
- Continuous rainfall  
- Warm temperature  

Farmers should:
- Monitor weather  
- Increase field inspection  
- Act early if symptoms appear  

================================================
11. SAFE USE OF CHEMICALS
================================================
- Use only recommended dose  
- Wear gloves and mask  
- Do not spray against wind  
- Keep chemicals away from children  
- Observe waiting period before harvest
