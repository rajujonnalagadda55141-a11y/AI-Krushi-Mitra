=== FERTILIZERS CORE GUIDE ===
Purpose: AI Krushi Mitra Knowledge Base
Scope: All crops

------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT ARE FERTILIZERS
------------------------------------------------
Fertilizers are substances that provide essential nutrients to plants for healthy growth, high yield, and good quality produce.

------------------------------------------------
2. MAIN NUTRIENTS
------------------------------------------------
Plants need 16 essential nutrients.

Primary nutrients:
- Nitrogen (N) – leaf growth
- Phosphorus (P) – root and flower development
- Potassium (K) – disease resistance and quality

Secondary nutrients:
- Calcium (Ca) – cell structure
- Magnesium (Mg) – chlorophyll
- Sulphur (S) – protein formation

Micronutrients:
- Zinc (Zn)
- Iron (Fe)
- Boron (B)
- Copper (Cu)
- Manganese (Mn)
- Molybdenum (Mo)
- Chlorine (Cl)

------------------------------------------------
3. TYPES OF FERTILIZERS
------------------------------------------------

A. Chemical fertilizers  
- Urea (Nitrogen source)  
- DAP (Nitrogen + Phosphorus)  
- MOP (Potassium)  
- SSP (Phosphorus + Sulphur)  
- NPK mixtures  

B. Organic fertilizers  
- Farm Yard Manure (FYM)  
- Compost  
- Vermicompost  
- Green manure  

C. Bio-fertilizers  
- Rhizobium  
- Azotobacter  
- Azospirillum  
- Phosphate Solubilizing Bacteria (PSB)  

------------------------------------------------
4. METHODS OF APPLICATION
------------------------------------------------

1. Basal application  
Applied before or at sowing.

2. Top dressing  
Applied during crop growth.

3. Foliar spray  
Sprayed on leaves for quick effect.

4. Fertigation  
Applied through irrigation water.

------------------------------------------------
5. TIMING OF FERTILIZER USE
------------------------------------------------
Critical stages:
- Early growth stage  
- Flowering stage  
- Grain / fruit formation stage  

Avoid applying fertilizers:
- During heavy rain  
- During drought stress  
- In very hot afternoon

------------------------------------------------
6. SOIL TESTING
------------------------------------------------
Soil testing helps to:
- Know nutrient deficiency  
- Avoid overuse of fertilizer  
- Reduce cost  
- Protect soil health  

Recommendation:
- Test soil once every 2–3 years.

------------------------------------------------
7. BALANCED FERTILIZATION
------------------------------------------------
Balanced use means:
- Not using only urea  
- Using N, P, K in correct ratio  
- Adding micronutrients if needed  

Overuse problems:
- Soil becomes hard  
- More pests and diseases  
- Water pollution  
- Lower yield in long term  

------------------------------------------------
8. COMMON NUTRIENT DEFICIENCY SYMPTOMS
------------------------------------------------

Nitrogen deficiency:
- Yellowing of old leaves  
- Slow growth  

Phosphorus deficiency:
- Poor root growth  
- Purple color in leaves  

Potassium deficiency:
- Leaf edge drying  
- Weak plants  

Zinc deficiency:
- White or yellow streaks on leaves  

Iron deficiency:
- Yellow young leaves, green veins  

------------------------------------------------
9. FERTILIZER SAFETY
------------------------------------------------
- Store fertilizers in dry place  
- Keep away from children  
- Do not mix different fertilizers unless advised  
- Wear gloves while handling  
- Wash hands after use  

------------------------------------------------
10. ORGANIC VS CHEMICAL – BEST PRACTICE
------------------------------------------------
Best farming uses both:

- Organic fertilizers improve soil  
- Chemical fertilizers give quick nutrients  
- Bio-fertilizers improve nutrient uptake  

Integrated Nutrient Management (INM):
Using all three together for long-term soil health.

------------------------------------------------
11. FERTILIZERS AND ENVIRONMENT
------------------------------------------------
Problems from misuse:
- Water pollution  
- Soil degradation  
- Greenhouse gas emissions  

Solutions:
- Use only recommended amount  
- Apply at right time  
- Use drip fertigation  
- Add organic matter  

------------------------------------------------
12. GOVERNMENT & FARMER ADVICE
------------------------------------------------
- Buy fertilizers only from licensed shops  
- Check subsidy eligibility  
- Follow agriculture officer advice  
- Use soil health card recommendations
